Half-Day vs. Full-Day Lake Lanier Sailing Charter: Which to Pick

Captain John Rice · 2026-09-05

By Captain John Rice, USCG-licensed 50-ton Master, Captain & Owner, Lord Nelson Charters, 36-ft sloop, Aqualand Marina · 2026-09-04 · 9 min read

Two couples called me last month with the same question: is a half day sailing charter lake lanier trip enough, or should we spring for the seven-hour package? One booked the four-hour sail for a Saturday afternoon. The other picked the full day for a milestone anniversary and dropped anchor in a back cove past Two Mile Creek. Both left the boat happy, but for very different reasons. This guide walks you through both.

Key takeaways

  • A half day sailing charter lake lanier trip runs $750 for two guests over four hours on the water, with each additional guest at $100.
  • The full-day sail runs $1,050 for two guests across seven hours, with additional guests at $150 each.
  • Effective per-hour cost for a couple drops from $187.50 on the half day to $150 on the full day.
  • Lake Lanier stretches across 38,000 acres and 692 miles of shoreline, per the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers.
  • Both packages depart from Aqualand Marina, Dock Zk, off I-985 near Flowery Branch, Georgia.
  • Peak-season Saturdays should be reserved four to eight weeks out per Lake Lanier Association reservation guidance.

What a half day sailing charter lake lanier package actually includes

A half day sailing charter lake lanier trip with Lord Nelson Charters is four hours on the water, captained personally by me from Aqualand Marina, Dock Zk. Two guests are included at $750; each additional guest is $100 up to the boat's permitted group size. You bring food and drink; I provide the sails, safety gear, and route.

The four hours starts when you step aboard, not when we clear the marina, so we tend to cast off within fifteen minutes of your arrival. A typical half-day rotation looks like a rigging walkthrough, a sail out past the Aqualand breakwater, an hour of open-water sailing across the main lake, an optional short anchor stop for a swim, and the return sail. Life jackets, coolers with ice, a Bluetooth speaker, and clean towels are on board.

Half day sailing charter lake lanier vs. seven hours: pricing and per-hour value

The half day sailing charter lake lanier package looks cheaper on the sticker, but the math flips when you account for time. Four hours at $750 works out to $187.50 per hour for a couple. Seven hours at $1,050 works out to $150 per hour for the same two guests. That is a 20% drop in effective rate.

The gap widens with more guests. A four-person half-day comes to $950 total ($750 base plus two extra at $100 each); the same four-person full-day comes to $1,350 ($1,050 base plus two extra at $150). Per-person per-hour on the half day is $59.38, while the full day works out to $48.21. Having run private charters on Lake Lanier since 2003, I recommend the full day for milestone celebrations or any group planning a mid-lake anchor-and-swim stop as the centerpiece. If you are testing the water for the first time or building the sail into a larger day in the Atlanta metro region, the half day is the right call.

Who should book a half day sailing charter lake lanier over the full sail

A half day sailing charter lake lanier fits best for first-time sailors, families with kids under age eight, guests with limited mobility, or anyone squeezing the sail into an afternoon between other Atlanta commitments. The four-hour window is long enough to raise sails and cross open water, but short enough that fatigue and sunburn stay manageable.

For families, four hours also happens to be the sweet spot for young attention spans. Kids under about eight tend to run out of interest around the three-hour mark, which is when we start turning back on the half day anyway. Parents worried about the specifics of bringing children aboard should read our guide on sailing with kids on Lake Lanier. For guests with mobility concerns, the shorter package limits time on a moving deck and keeps the trip comfortable. The U.S. Coast Guard boating safety program notes that fatigue is a leading contributor to on-water incidents, and four hours puts far less strain on everyone.

Anchorages and swim stops that need a full day, not four hours

Lake Lanier spans 38,000 acres and 692 miles of shoreline, per U.S. Army Corps of Engineers Mobile District figures. Reaching the quieter back coves past Browns Bridge, several nautical miles north-northwest, or the wildlife channels near Two Mile Creek on a northeast bearing requires transit time that does not fit inside a four-hour window.

Aqualand Marina sits at the southern end of the reservoir, with the main sailing channel running north toward Buford Dam. The seven-hour package builds in that travel budget while still leaving time to drop anchor in protected water, run the swim ladder, and eat lunch on deck before the return sail back to Flowery Branch. The four-hour trip stays within the southern stretch of the lake near Aqualand and offers a shorter optional swim stop rather than a dedicated anchor-and-lunch sequence.

On a full day I plan around a mid-lake anchor stop, usually in a protected cove roughly 45 to 60 minutes out under sail. Guests jump off the swim ladder, float on noodles, and eat lunch on deck. We then sail farther north, sometimes as far as the Buford Dam side, before turning back. That distance is simply not available on the half day; the round-trip travel budget alone eats the four hours. If you want the specifics of what a full day looks like end to end, I wrote a full breakdown in the seven-hour charter guide.

How to book your half day sailing charter lake lanier: timing and weather policy

Peak season on Lake Lanier runs April through October. Peak-season Saturdays book four to eight weeks ahead, per Lake Lanier Association guidance. For a peak-season half day sailing charter lake lanier booking, reserve at least four weeks out. Weekday sails open two weeks out. Sunset windows book fastest in July.

Weather cancellations follow National Weather Service Peachtree City forecasts for wind, storms, and lightning. I make the go/no-go call the morning of the sail. Cancellations get rescheduled at no extra cost. If you are trying to line up your booking with the calmest months, check the month-by-month sail guide. For sunset-specific timing (which is its own category), the sunset sail guide has departure windows.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a half day sailing charter lake lanier trip cost?

Lord Nelson Charters prices the half-day package at $750 for two guests over four hours on the water. Each additional guest is $100. The boat's permitted group size caps how many people you can bring; that number varies by boat and season. Prices are current as of publish date; the live rate sheet on the pricing page is authoritative. Compared to going out with a larger Atlanta metro operator, this rate covers the whole boat privately (no shared seat) and the captain is with you the whole sail.

What is the actual difference between the half-day and full-day sail?

Three hours of on-water time and $300 total for a couple. The half day sailing charter lake lanier trip is four hours at $750; the full day is seven hours at $1,050. Beyond time and price, the full day gives real range: I can reach the back coves past Two Mile Creek, run north toward the Buford Dam side, and drop a proper anchor for a 90-minute swim. The half day stays closer to marina waters and typically only allows a short swim stop. On group size, additional guests are $100 each on the half day, $150 each on the full day. Per-person value per hour favors the full day above two guests.

Can I bring alcohol and food on the charter?

Yes. Both packages are bring-your-own for food and drink, including beer, wine, and cocktails. There is a small galley cooler on board with ice, but I recommend bringing your own cooler for larger parties. Georgia boating law (enforced on Lake Lanier by the Georgia Department of Natural Resources) prohibits open containers being used by the operator, so all alcohol stays with guests, not with the captain. For a full packing list including cooler size, footwear, and sunscreen guidance, see the packing guide.

What happens if the weather cancels my charter?

Weather cancellations are rescheduled at no charge. I check National Weather Service Peachtree City forecasts the morning of your sail and make the call by 8am. If lightning, sustained winds above roughly 20 knots, or severe storm systems are in the forecast, we reschedule. Light rain by itself is not usually a cancellation trigger; I will call and give you the choice. Deposits roll to the new date. If your date flexibility is limited (a proposal, a birthday), I usually recommend booking Saturday morning slots because they get the calmest air and the most reschedule options within the same week.

How many guests can fit on the sailboat?

The boat is permitted for a specific total group size that I confirm when you book. For most charters, we plan around two to six guests as the comfort range; larger groups can be accommodated up to the permitted cap. Both the half-day and full-day packages start with two guests included in the base rate ($750 and $1,050 respectively). Additional guests are billed at $100 per person on the half day and $150 per person on the full day. For a half day sailing charter lake lanier booking with over eight guests, please message me directly so I can confirm the boat and seating for your specific date.

Where does the charter depart from?

Every charter departs from Aqualand Marina, Dock Zk, in Flowery Branch, Georgia. It is about 45 minutes north of downtown Atlanta off I-985 exit 8, then a short drive down Aqualand Road. Free guest parking is available in the marina lot. I meet you at the dock 15 minutes before your slot for a safety walkthrough and to help load coolers. On arrival, follow signs to Dock Zk at the back of the marina parking area. Both the half day sailing charter lake lanier package and the full-day trip depart from Dock Zk year-round.
